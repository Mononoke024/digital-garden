A new mantra I repeat to myself that encourages me to go forth and explore new experiences.

I'd never equated one with the other until chancing upon Memoircomics' Youtube video: [How to follow your dream when you don't have one](https://youtu.be/w46fYAfIgg4?si=_WcJ4i8ZelHa52OH) (11:09 - 19:30)

I've always feared death, but didn't realise that this fear of dying, of failure, of being perceived was in essence a way that I had been avoiding life.

I convinced myself that I had to make stark changes to pursue a meaningful life, all while fearing the end. Now it seems silly, and that it didn't make any sense. 

*Life and death are two sides of the same coin.*

To live life in pursuit of what brings you joy, and to have fun while doing it, means you're willingly escaping the fear of death.

Taking risks is necessary, and taking risks is part of the journey, see: [[Quotes#Anthony Bourdain on taking risks]]
